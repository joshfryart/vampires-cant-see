Air rings and vibrating surfaces

Looking down a 5 by 13 corridor: crate on the left, a villager just past it, a pillar on the right, a doorway further down the right wall. The ping is a mouth beam aimed where you face (0/-1/-3/-8/-14/-18/-20 dB at 0 to 180 degrees). It shows in the air as rings expanding outward and fading, drawn at chest and head height so you see their arcs float in perspective. Where a ring reaches a wall or an object, that surface is lit only at the contact and shivers: each surface point vibrates as a damped sine clocked from the moment the front passed it, so the shiver spreads outward from the nearest contact point. Objects and people take a warmer tint.

Default 5 cells/s. Auto-pings when the previous ring has gone.
